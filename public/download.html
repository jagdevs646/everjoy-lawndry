Everjoy on your phone.

The app has the same screens as the website: your digital slip, the QR for collecting, and your confirmation codes. You sign in with the same account.

Android

Download the app and install it. It is not on the Play Store yet, so your phone asks you to allow the install once.

Download for Android

APK file, about 7 MB. Android 7 or newer.

  1. Tap Download for Android. If Chrome warns that the file can be harmful, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file. When asked, allow Install unknown apps for Chrome (or your Files app), then go back.
  3. Tap Install, then Open, and sign in.

To update, download it again and install it over the old one. You stay signed in.

iPhone

The iPhone app is not on the App Store yet. Add Everjoy to your home screen instead: it opens full screen from its own icon, like an app.

Open Everjoy in Safari
  1. In Safari, tap Open Everjoy in Safari above.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Open Everjoy from the new icon and sign in.
